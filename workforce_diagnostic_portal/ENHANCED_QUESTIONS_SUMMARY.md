## Question Design Principles

### 🎯 Strong & Meaningful Questions
- **Specific to Role**: Each question addresses unique responsibilities of the role
- **Decision-Oriented**: Questions evaluate critical decision-making aspects
- **Impact-Focused**: Questions measure real business impact
- **Actionable**: Results provide clear guidance for decisions

### 📊 Evaluation Metrics
- **Scale Questions**: 1-5 scale for nuanced assessment
- **Choice Questions**: Multiple choice for clear categorization
- **Boolean Questions**: Yes/No for binary decisions
- **Help Text**: Clear guidance for consistent evaluation

### 🔍 System Evaluation Effectiveness
- **360-Degree View**: Questions cover all aspects of role responsibilities
- **Risk Assessment**: Built-in risk evaluation for each role
- **Strategic Alignment**: Questions link to strategic business goals
- **Operational Readiness**: Practical evaluation of implementation capability
